## Discussion and Decisions

- The project will use a fullstack Next.js application: UI and route handlers in the same app, with two servers for the P2P scenario.
- Tailwind CSS and shadcn/ui are the styling choice.
- Authentication will use a JWT in an httpOnly cookie; role enforcement remains server-side.
- PostgreSQL is the shared development and production database.
- A simple custom hash-linked chain will represent access logs; medical records and the group contract remain outside the blockchain.
- The team will work with short-lived feature branches, pull requests, code review, and CI checks for application code.
- The default meeting cadence is a Tuesday quick sync and a Thursday progress review, with notes published in `docs/meetings/` on the meeting day.
- The backend/UI work is split into streams, but final stream ownership is revisited as the project progresses.
